### Payment app

- Everyone is the public transaction feed. Friends is the contacts feed. Mine is the signed-in user's transaction feed.
- A payment sends money. A request asks another person to send money.

### Moodle

- A course is a space for learning materials and activities. An activity is an item within a course, such as an assignment.
- The Dashboard is the user's start page. A block is a panel that can appear on a Moodle page.
